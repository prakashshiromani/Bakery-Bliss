Our Story

Born in a
Small Kitchen.

It started with a grandmother's recipe and a dream. Bakery Bliss was founded in a tiny home kitchen in 2018 with one simple belief — great bread changes your day.

Today, we bake over 1,000 items every morning, but our soul remains the same: every loaf, every pastry, every cake is made by hand, with patience and passion.
